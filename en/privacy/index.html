Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Rivon Labs (the “Company”) values the privacy of its users and complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Snowly, a Mac app made by the Company (the “App”), and this website (the “Website”).

1. Information the App Collects

The App does not collect or transmit any data, including personal information. To find where snow can settle, it reads only the position and size of other apps’ windows on your Mac and which app owns them. It does not read window titles, window contents, screen images, or the state of your microphone or input devices. The App does not connect to the internet or request any permissions, and its settings are stored only on your Mac.

2. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

The App does not display any advertisements and does not use any third-party services or SDKs that collect, share, or analyze user data.

3. Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to analyze visits to the Website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how you use the Website, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, your device and browser, and your approximate location. It does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address. This information is processed on Google’s servers, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States, and individual usage records are deleted after two months. We use this information only to improve the Website, never for advertising.

To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Website Hosting

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

5. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the address below. We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete them one year after we reply, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. If you would like them deleted sooner, email us at the address below, and we will delete them without delay unless the law requires us to keep them.

Rivon Labs
Email: contact@rivonlabs.com

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will notify you on this page and update the effective date.

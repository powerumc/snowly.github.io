Frequently asked questions

How do I turn it off?

Use Option-click to turn snow and sound on or off: hold Option and click the snowflake icon in the menu bar. You can also press ⌃⌥⌘S. To quit the app, choose Quit Snowly (⌘Q) from the icon’s menu.

Does it snow in full screen too?

No. Snow pauses on a screen showing a full-screen app.

What if I have more than one display?

Snow falls on each display separately, and you can turn it off for each one.

Does it need permissions or an internet connection?

No. It asks for no permissions, needs no internet connection, and collects no data.

What information does it read?

To find where snow can settle, it reads only the position and size of other apps’ windows and which app owns them.
